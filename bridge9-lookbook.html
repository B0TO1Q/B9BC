Where the fade starts
Taper
Gets gradually shorter but keeps hair on the sides. Cleans up only the sideburns and neckline.
Fade
Blends all the way down to skin or a bare blade. The higher it starts, the bolder it reads.
Ask in three parts
or just point to the page.
1 · Sides
Guard number, plus taper or fade and how high it starts.
2 · Top
Length in inches to keep, and the texture: choppy, smooth or slicked.
3 · Finish
Neckline (tapered, blocked or rounded), line-up and beard.

Tapers & Fades
01

Low Taper

The clean classic.

Natural and work-ready. Length stays on the sides and only tightens up at the sideburns and neckline. Easy to grow out.

Sides#2–#3, tapering down low
Top2–3" scissor cut, light texture
FinishNeckline tapered to a #½
Say it like this"Low taper, number 2 on the sides, scissor on top, clean up the neckline."
Upkeep · 3 weeksProduct · light cream
02

Mid Skin Fade

Sharp from every angle.

Skin at the temple line, blending up to length mid-head. The most requested cut in the shop. Pairs with any top.

SidesSkin → #1 → #2 → #3
Top1½–3", textured
FinishLine-up optional
Say it like this"Mid skin fade, blend up to a 3, about two inches textured on top."
Upkeep · 2 weeksProduct · matte clay

Short & Sharp
03

High & Tight

Squared away.

Military roots. The sides come off short almost up to the crown, leaving a tight strip of length on top. Zero fuss in the morning.

SidesSkin or #½ → #1, faded high
TopAbout 1", clipper or scissor
FinishBlocked or tapered neckline
Say it like this"High and tight, zero to a 1 high on the sides, leave about an inch on top."
Upkeep · 2 weeksProduct · none needed
04

Buzz Cut

All shape, no styling.

One guard all over with the edges cleaned up. Shows off your head shape and takes care of itself between visits.

SidesSame guard as the top
TopYour pick, #1 to #4
FinishTapered neckline, clean around ears
Say it like this"Buzz with a 3 all over, taper the neckline and clean up around the ears."
Upkeep · 2–3 weeksProduct · none

Crops & Classics
05

Classic Crew Cut

The all-American standard.

Short tapered sides. The top is a little longer at the front and graduates shorter toward the crown. Looks right in a uniform or a T-shirt.

Sides#3 tapering to a #2
Top1½" at the front, shorter to the crown
FinishTapered neckline
Say it like this"Crew cut, 3 tapering to a 2, about an inch and a half at the front."
Upkeep · 3–4 weeksProduct · matte paste
06

French Crop

Choppy up top, clean below.

Faded sides with a textured top pushed forward into a short, blunt fringe. Great for thick hair or a receding hairline.

SidesMid fade, skin or #½ up to #2
Top2–3", point-cut texture, pushed forward
FinishStraight, blunt fringe
Say it like this"French crop, mid fade, two to three inches on top, textured, blunt fringe."
Upkeep · 3 weeksProduct · sea salt + clay

Gentleman's Cuts
07

Pompadour

Height with intent.

Volume up front, swept up and back, with tapered sides keeping the shape sharp. Needs length on top and a blow-dryer at home.

Sides#2–#3 taper, kept tight
Top4–6", longest at the front
FinishBlow-dried up and back
Say it like this"Pompadour, number 2 taper on the sides, leave four to six inches on top, longest in front."
Upkeep · 4 weeksProduct · pomade
08

Side Part

Boardroom ready.

Tapered sides with the top combed over a clean part. Ask for a hard part and your barber shaves the line in for definition.

Sides#2 taper, scissor-blended
Top3–4", combed to one side
FinishNatural or hard part
Say it like this"Side part, number 2 taper, three to four inches on top, hard part on the left."
Upkeep · 3 weeksProduct · medium-hold pomade

Signature Fades
09

Burst Fade

A halo around the ear.

The fade curves in a half-circle around the ear and leaves length at the back. Built for curls, twists and mohawk styles.

SidesSkin at the ear, bursting out to length
TopKeep full; curls or twists welcome
FinishLine-up at the front, length at the back
Say it like this"Burst fade around the ear, keep the length in the back, line up the front."
Upkeep · 2 weeksProduct · curl cream
10

Drop Fade

Curves where others go straight.

The fade line dips down behind the ear toward the nape, giving the back of the head a curved, deliberate silhouette.

SidesSkin → #2, dropped behind the ear
Top2–3", textured or curly
FinishRounded nape, line-up optional
Say it like this"Mid drop fade, skin up to a 2, dropped behind the ear, textured on top."
Upkeep · 2 weeksProduct · matte clay

Long Top & Beard
11

Slick Back Undercut

Sharp contrast, no blend.

Short, disconnected sides under a long top combed straight back. Bold, clean and easy to restyle loose on the weekend.

Sides#1–#2, hard line, no blend
Top5–7", combed straight back
FinishBlocked or tapered neckline
Say it like this"Undercut, number 1 on the sides with no blend, keep five to seven inches on top to slick back."
Upkeep · 3–4 weeksProduct · high-shine pomade
12

Beard Shape-Up

Finish the frame.

Cheek and neck lines carved clean, the beard faded up into the sideburn and the length trimmed even. Add it to any cut in this book.

CheekNatural line, cleaned with a razor
NeckA finger's width above the Adam's apple
BlendFaded from the sideburn into the beard
Say it like this"Beard shape-up, fade it into the sideburn, line the cheeks, keep the length even."
Upkeep · 1–2 weeksProduct · beard oil + balm
